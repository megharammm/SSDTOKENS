Counters (Offline SSD)

Rules (short)

  • Same-day darshan only. Tokens issued day-to-day till quota exhausts.
  • Aadhaar required, in-person, photo taken. Not transferable.
  • Below 12 years: no token needed.
  • Next SSD only after 30 days once availed.
  • Report at ATGH Circle at slot time on token. Free laddu after darshan at laddu complex.
  • If quota over: go to Vaikuntha Queue Complex-2 compartments for Sarva Darshan (no slot).

How to plan

  1. Reach Tirupati night before. Counters often open 5:00 AM, sometimes midnight if rush.
  2. Usually full by 7-8 AM. Go early with Aadhaar.
  3. If you miss: footpath Divya Darshan or VQC-2 queue.
  4. Festival / Brahmotsavam days: timings change. Check tirumala.org.

FAQ

Where to get tokens?

Vishnu Nivasam, Srinivasam Complex, Bhudevi Complex / Govindaraja Satralu in Tirupati. No footpath issue.

Free. After darshan one laddu free on token submission.
